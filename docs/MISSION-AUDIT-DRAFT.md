Pre-boundary pending requests are failed/retained (`:1640–1652`), and a later request can take fresh current inputs. **No static universal obstruction to fresh pre-effect local delivery was found.** The special race of narrowing an already queued/pre-boundary finish was not established by an inspected end-to-end test; that remains U, not a reproduced bug.
4. **After `deliveryRequestedAt` or an admitted remote integration**, narrowing cannot undo a push/PR/merge or relabel an old request's policy. It records the old operation and a reconciliation blocker. `mission-service.test.ts:1112–1153` intentionally waits for the crossed boundary, retains its real local commit and refuses Resume. `receiptInputs` rejects changed policy/authorization/content. That is correct conservative behavior; weakening it to make a late reduction pass would be unsafe.
5. A fresh pre-effect attempt is different from reconciling an old partial remote receipt. The latter is N3; it needs positive remote artifact inspection and exact ownership, not a new arbitrary idempotency key or a call to `deliver` at boot.
